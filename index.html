About

What I build

I build workflow backends and the AI agent layer of a manufacturing PLM platform: revision-safe engineering data models for drawings, BOMs, and change workflows; hybrid retrieval and graph grounding over 20,000+ technical documents with source citations; and on-prem LLM serving (SGLang) on customer GPUs where data cannot leave the site.

Alongside day-to-day product engineering, I built and operate a real-time multimodal AI agent SaaS on Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects (split-agent loop, MCP tool execution, per-tenant RAG isolation, and idempotent multi-provider billing), contributed bug fixes to NVIDIA/NVSentinel, and maintain open-source agent safety tooling (devin-harness).

The common thread across my work is verifiability: retrieval that cites exact source revisions, AI agents bounded by deterministic guardrails, and multi-tenant backends with strict isolation and auditable ledgers.
