About

What I do

I build production software end to end — backend services, frontend interfaces, databases, infrastructure, and the model/serving layer in between. Most of my work is AI systems, but the AI is one layer of a larger engineering problem: multi-tenant boundaries, payment correctness, retrieval quality, and the operational reality of keeping it all running.

Currently I'm a core engineer at an AI startup, owning backend modules and the AI layer of an enterprise platform: retrieval over large document corpora with per-scope isolation, knowledge-graph grounding, and LLM serving on customer-owned GPUs — where the data legally can't leave the building, so the model, retrieval stack, and quality gates are all operated in-house.

Before that I designed, built, and operate a multi-tenant AI SaaS solo — real-time multimodal sessions, per-user knowledge stores, a payment ledger with idempotent billing webhooks, and usage metering. Before that, contract work shipping applied-AI products: an LLM support system serving 5,000+ members, and data pipelines that had to stay correct without manual oversight.

What I'm good at

LLM infrastructure & serving. On-prem inference on customer GPUs — vLLM/SGLang, quantization, KV-cache and prefix-cache tuning, guided decoding for structured/cited output, golden-set regression testing. I write about this: the on-prem serving field guide.
Retrieval systems. Hybrid sparse+dense retrieval with reranking, knowledge graphs, per-tenant isolation enforced server-side, and the honest limits of each approach. GraphRAG teardown: e-commerce catalogs · multi-tenant isolation.
Agent systems. I extracted and open-sourced devin-harness — lifecycle hooks, deterministic guards, action ledgers, typed review gates — because in production, the harness around an agent determines whether it's safe to leave unsupervised. Teardown.
Payments, billing & SaaS operations. Double-entry ledgers, idempotency keys, webhook reconciliation, quotas and entitlements — the unglamorous systems where correctness is the product.
Full-stack ownership. React/TypeScript frontends, FastAPI/Node/Spring Boot backends, PostgreSQL/Redis/Kafka, AWS/GCP/Cloudflare, Terraform and CI/CD — I've owned complete products alone, which means I know where the seams are.
